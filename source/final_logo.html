The brief01

The script of option three,
the colour of the door.

The final mark keeps the hand-written wordmark and the slogan exactly as drawn: à la porte — A TRAY FOR EVERYDAY, with the hand presenting the tray. It is now set in Rouge Porte on Parchemin: a warm terracotta on a sun-washed cream, like a painted door in a plaster wall.

Anatomy05
i.
The wordmark

Hand-lettered script in the spirit of a written invitation. Always used as artwork, never re-typed.

ii.
The slogan

A TRAY FOR EVERYDAY: tracked capitals tucked beneath the script, as drawn. The descender of the p threads through it.

iii.
The hand

The maître d’s gesture. A gloved hand presents the tray, the brand’s promise of service at your door.

Mostly wall, some door, and a little garden.

Parchemin and Ivoire carry the space. Rouge Porte is the voice: the logo, a headline, a ribbon. Bordeaux and Olive add depth and seasonality; Café is for reading. Laiton appears only as foil, never as flat ink.

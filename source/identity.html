The idea

01

À la porte means
“at the door.”

It is the moment the doorbell rings and something beautiful arrives: a tray, arranged by hand, presented as a maître d’ would present it. Not a delivery, an arrival.

The identity is built from two objects: the door, where every order ends, and the hand that carries it there. Everything else is warmth, restraint and a little old-world ceremony, for every day.

Tone of voice

09

Warm like a host,
never loud.

We speak the way a good host greets you at the door: short, warm, a touch of French. One French word per line at most, always easy to read. Never shout prices, never use more than one emoji.
